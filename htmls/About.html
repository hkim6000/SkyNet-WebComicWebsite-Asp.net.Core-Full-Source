About Panelmoon

Comics that turn the page.

Panelmoon is a home for daily and weekly comic series by independent artists. Every episode opens in a page-flipping reader that works on a desktop, a tablet or a phone.

How it works

1

Artists publish

Artists drag in their pages, put them in order and pick a daily or weekly schedule in Artist Studio.

2

Readers flip

Every episode opens like a book: two-page spreads on wide screens, single pages with a swipe on phones.

3

Everyone talks

Readers like, dislike and comment on every episode, and subscribe to hear when the next one drops.
